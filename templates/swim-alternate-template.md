# Swim-Alternate Session Template
*Used when a swim day is blocked by weather. Never replaces Gym A or Gym B.*

## Purpose
Fill the calorie-burn role of the swim (~350–450 kcal active) without adding hypertrophy stimulus. Cardio-dominant, conditioning-based. ~55–60 min total.

## Equipment
Cardio machines (bike / rower / treadmill), battle ropes, sled (push + pull), core mat.

---

## Base Structure (all variants follow this skeleton)

| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 8 min | Cardio machine — easy pace, HR < 120 BPM |
| Cardio Block | 15–18 min | See variant below |
| Battle Ropes | ~12 min | 3–4 rounds, see variant below |
| Sled | ~12 min | 3–4 rounds push + pull |
| Core | 8–10 min | See core menu |
| Cool-down | 3–5 min | Walk / stretch |

---

## Variant A — After Upper Push (Tue, when Mon was Gym A)
*Upper pushing muscles (chest, shoulders, triceps) are 24h post-lift. Protect them.*

**Cardio Block (15 min):**
- Assault bike or rower — 5 × 2 min hard / 1 min easy. Drive with legs, not arms on the rower. Bike preferred.

**Battle Ropes (3 rounds, 60s rest between):**
- Alternating waves × 30s — keep arm motion minimal, drive from hips
- Rope slams × 20 reps — hip hinge dominant, arms just guide
- Lateral shuffle + waves × 20s each direction
- *Skip any overhead or bilateral pull pattern — shoulders are already loaded.*

**Sled (3 rounds, 90s rest):**
- Sled push × 20m — leg drive, low stance
- Sled pull (rope hand-over-hand) × 20m — acceptable here as it's bicep-dominant not tricep/shoulder
- Rest, then repeat

**Core (pick 3):**
- Dead bug × 10 each side
- Hollow body hold × 30s
- Ab wheel rollout × 8–10
- Side plank × 30s each side

---

## Variant B — After Upper Pull (Thu, when Wed was Gym B)
*Back, biceps, rear delts are 24h post-lift. Protect them.*

**Cardio Block (18 min):**
- Treadmill incline walk or light jog — 3 min easy / 2 min fast × 3 rounds. Or rower with low drag (legs-only focus).

**Battle Ropes (4 rounds, 60s rest):**
- Alternating waves × 30s — full motion fine, back is warmed up not overtaxed
- Double slam × 15 reps
- In-out waves × 20s
- Rotational slam × 10 each side
- *All patterns fine — pull day muscles are support here, not primary movers.*

**Sled (4 rounds, 90s rest):**
- Sled push × 20m
- Sled push × 20m (repeat — more push than pull today to spare biceps/back)
- If recovery feels good: one sled drag (facing away, harness around waist) — hip extension dominant

**Core (pick 3):**
- Hanging knee raise × 12
- Plank × 45s
- Russian twist (bodyweight) × 20 reps
- Pallof press × 10 each side (if cable available)

---

## Variant C — Standalone (Fri or Sat, no gym day adjacent)
*No lifting fatigue to work around. Full session.*

**Cardio Block (18 min):**
- Rower — 500m hard / 90s rest × 3 rounds. Or 20 min bike intervals (30s hard / 90s easy).

**Battle Ropes (4 rounds, 60s rest):**
- Alternating waves × 30s
- Double slam × 20 reps
- Lateral shuffle + waves × 20s each direction
- Overhead slam × 10 reps (both arms)
- Sprint + rope wave combo if space allows

**Sled (4 rounds, 90s rest):**
- Sled push × 20m
- Sled pull (rope) × 20m
- Repeat

**Core (pick 3–4):**
- Ab wheel × 10
- Hanging knee raise × 12
- Plank × 60s
- Dead bug × 10 each side
- Side plank × 30s each

## Quick Reference — Which Variant to Use

| Blocked Day | Previous Gym Day | Use Variant |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | Mon = Gym A (Push) | **A** — protect shoulders/triceps |
| Thu | Wed = Gym B (Pull) | **B** — protect back/biceps |
| Fri | Thu = swim or swim-alt | **C** — full session |
| Sat | Fri = swim or swim-alt | **C** — full session |

---

## RPE Target
Keep effort at **RPE 6–7** across the session. This is a calorie-burn day, not a conditioning PB day. If effort climbs to 8+, back off the sled or rest longer between rounds.

## Notes
- No direct leg isolation work (no lunges, leg press, leg curl). Sled and rower incidentally load legs — that's fine.
- If post-swim soreness is already high on a blocked day, default to Variant A regardless of what day it is — reduce sled to 2 rounds and drop one battle rope round.
- Log: time, approx calories, effort rating (same as swim logging format).
